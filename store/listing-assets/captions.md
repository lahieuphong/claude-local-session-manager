# Screenshot captions

Paste each caption into the caption field of the matching screenshot in
Partner Center → Store listings → *language* → Screenshots. Partner Center
allows at most 200 characters per caption (`tests/storeListingAssets.test.ts`
checks this). The wording follows the app's own UI terms in each language.
